Wave is in: companies

SpendClean is a public directory of company political giving records.

We look at public records to see what they show about company political action committees and political contributions. Each company gets one of three labels: clean-verified, flagged, or not yet verified. Every entry identifies its source and shows the record behind the label.

Start with the clean-verified list below, or look up a company you shop with. Read what we found, check the source, and decide what matters to you.

The clean-verified companies: all of them

For these companies, we found no corporate PAC and no corporate contributions in the records we reviewed for the election cycle shown on each company page. That is a finding about the records checked, not a guarantee about every kind of political activity a company or its affiliates may engage in. The quoted source sits next to each name, and the name opens its full page with the record.

How to read the marks

Clean-verified
We found no corporate PAC and no corporate contributions in the records we checked.
Flagged
We found a corporate PAC. Where the record states an amount, we copy it word for word. One company stays flagged on an older catalog record even though no committee for it appears in the current FEC registry; its page says so plainly.
Not yet verified
We started, but a question is still open, often which legal name a company files under. Never shown as clean.
