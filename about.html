About SpendClean

SpendClean is a public directory of company political giving records. Every entry shows its source, quoted on its page, and one of three labels: clean-verified, flagged, or not yet verified.

Most of us never see where a company's political money goes. It is all public, filed with the FEC and summarized by groups like OpenSecrets, but it sits in committee reports and spreadsheets that nobody has an evening to read before buying groceries. So we read them, write down what they say, and put it on a shelf where anyone can find it.

That is the whole idea. You look up a store, a bank, or the airline you fly home on, and you see one of three marks, the record it came from, and the date we checked.

You get the record. You make the call.

Who it is for

People who want to shop with their values in mind, whatever those values are. Some readers want to keep their money away from corporate PACs entirely. Some just want to know. Both are welcome here.

What it is not

SpendClean is a public library, not a lecture. We will never tell you where to shop. It is also not legal or financial advice. A mark describes what turned up in the records we checked, and the methodology says exactly what that covers and what it cannot.

How it grows

We add companies in waves, and every wave carries its own date. If there is a company you would like us to look up, or a page you think we got wrong, send it our way.
